#|Column|Description
---|---|---
1|Order_ID|Unique identifier assigned to each sales order.
2|Customer_ID|Synthetic identifier representing the customer associated with the transaction.
3|Customer_Age|Age of the customer associated with the transaction.
4|Customer_Gender|Gender of the customer associated with the transaction.
5|Customer_Segment|Customer segment or category assigned based on customer characteristics or purchasing behavior.
6|Loyalty_Member|Indicates whether the customer is a member of the bakery's loyalty program.
7|Date|Date on which the sales transaction occurred.
8|Year|Year in which the transaction occurred.
9|Month|Month in which the transaction occurred.
10|Day|Day of the month on which the transaction occurred.
11|Day_of_Week|Day of the week on which the transaction occurred.
12|Weekend|Indicates whether the transaction occurred on a weekend.
13|Hour|Hour of the day when the transaction occurred.
14|Time|Time period associated with the transaction.
15|Season|Season associated with the transaction date.
16|Festival|Festival or special occasion associated with the transaction period, where applicable.
17|Weather|Weather condition recorded or simulated for the transaction period.
18|Temperature|Temperature associated with the transaction period.
19|Product|Name of the bakery product involved in the transaction.
20|Category|Product category to which the bakery item belongs.
21|Quantity|Number of units purchased in the transaction.
22|Unit_Price|Price associated with one unit of the product before applicable adjustments.
23|Cost_Price|Cost associated with one unit of the product.
24|Discount_Percentage|Percentage discount applied to the product or transaction.
25|Selling_Price|Selling price associated with the product after applicable pricing or discount adjustments.
26|Total_Bill|Total monetary value of the customer's transaction.
27|Payment_Method|Payment method used to complete the transaction.
28|Promotion_Applied|Indicates whether a promotional offer was applied to the transaction.
29|Promotion_Type|Type of promotion applied to the transaction, where applicable.
30|Shelf_Life_Days|Expected shelf life of the product measured in days.
31|Storage_Type|Storage method or storage condition associated with the product.
32|Perishability|Perishability characteristic of the product, indicating its susceptibility to spoilage.
33|Store_ID|Identifier of the store where the transaction occurred.
34|Units_Produced|Number of units of the product produced or made available during the relevant period.
35|Units_Sold|Number of units of the product sold during the relevant period.
36|Unsold_Units|Number of produced units that remained unsold.
37|Stock_Available|Quantity of product stock available for sale.
38|Waste_Pct|Percentage of product inventory represented as waste or unsold/spoiled inventory.
39|Profit|Profit associated with the transaction or product sales.
40|Customer_Rating|Rating provided by the customer for the transaction or product experience.
